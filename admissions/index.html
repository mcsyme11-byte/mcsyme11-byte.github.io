How I work

And how I use AI

I build with AI, and I say so. Claude writes most of the code. My part is deciding what is worth building, writing the plan, judging the design, testing on real flights, and refusing to ship what isn't good enough.

It has taught me a lot about how engineering is really done: requirements, testing, release discipline, honest documentation, and what to do when something breaks.

What I want to study

Aerospace engineering, then a career in Canada's space industry.
