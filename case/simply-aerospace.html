01The idea

Every explainer takes a common misconception (the myth) and replaces it with what actually happens (the truth), in under a minute. Each topic is pitched at one of three levels, so the same channel works for pure curiosity and for students who want the real maths.

  • Junior Engineer: no math.
  • Flight Engineer: one equation.
  • Senior Engineer: real equations and worked examples.

02What I built

The explainers

24 short videos on aviation, space and propulsion, each a 5-slide deck: hook, myth, concept, payoff, outro.

The website

A small encyclopedia of the channel: every explainer has an article with the myth, the truth and a way to go deeper.

Mission Control

Stories for ages 7 to 11 with real choices: a briefing, decisions with consequences, then a debrief. Each teaches one skill.

Finding things

Search on every page, filters by topic and level, a myth index, a quiz and a glossary.

03Key decisions

Three levels, one channel

A level badge on every explainer tells people what to expect, from no math at all to worked examples.

Instagram first

The channel started on YouTube and Instagram. YouTube was paused after music was flagged and Shorts were capped at 60 seconds, so Instagram became its main home.

A fixed five-slide format

Hook, the myth, the concept, the payoff, the outro. Slides are drawn as SVG and rendered at 1080 × 1920, then assembled into a short video.

Pitch, then wait

Topics are pitched two to four at a time, balanced across subjects and levels. Nothing is built until I approve it.

No mistakes

Facts are checked before they're stated, against primary sources such as NASA, NOAA and ESA.

One look across my projects

Version 3.0 of the website moved to the same navy, brass and cream look as my other projects, using one typeface and no web fonts.

04What went wrong

Right after the redesign went live, the top bar scrolled away with the page, and the reading-progress line floated by itself. One line of CSS meant to stop sideways scrolling had quietly broken the sticky bar. Version 3.1.0 fixed it the same day.

What it taught me

Check the whole page after a redesign, not only the new parts. The website's repository now runs an automatic site check, and every release follows a written checklist.

05What I learned

Explaining something at three levels shows me whether I really understand it: if I can't say it with no math, I don't. And a myth is a better hook than a fact, because people stay to find out why what they believed is wrong.
