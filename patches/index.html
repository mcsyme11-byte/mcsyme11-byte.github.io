Mission patches

Every project flies its own patch.

Like real space missions, each of my projects has a mission patch, and six projects use theirs as their official logo. Select one to turn it over.

Anatomy of a patch

What each part means
  1. A

    The brass rim and stitching

    The same on every patch, so they read as one family.

  2. B

    The name on the ring

    The project's name, lettered around the top.

  3. C

    The centre symbol

    One simple picture of what the project is.

  4. D

    The inner ring's colour

    Green for current work, blue for past work, brass for ideas.

  5. E

    The year, between two stars

    The year it started, or "IDEA" until it does.
