About

Where the site meets the sky

I'm a student in Alberta, Canada, a certified drone pilot and the founder of my own company. Most of what I do starts with a question, like how a winglet saves fuel or how a controller keeps twenty aircraft apart, and ends as something real that other people can use.

In 2026 alone that meant the Flight Deck Suite (seven connected flight-sim apps, with a public release), nine live tools built on NASA and ESA data, a dictation app that runs entirely on your own computer, and 24 aerospace explainers. The goal is aerospace engineering, then Canada's space industry. I'm not waiting to get started.
