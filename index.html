About

Where the site meets the sky

I'm a student in Alberta, Canada, with a passion for aviation and space. Most of what I do starts with a question, like how a winglet saves fuel or how a controller keeps twenty aircraft apart, and turns into something I can build, test and share.

My goal is to study aerospace engineering and work in Canada's space industry. Until then, I'm learning by doing: writing real software, flying drones commercially, building rockets and teaching what I learn.
