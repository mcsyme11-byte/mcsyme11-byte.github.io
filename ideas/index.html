Idea Lab

Thirty ideas. Seven already live.

One evening in November 2026 I wrote down thirty project ideas, then grouped them into seven bigger projects. Two days later, the first seven were live.

How an idea becomes real

Orbit & Space Watch took two days
  1. 01

    Write it down

    Thirty ideas on one list, on November 15, 2026.

  2. 02

    Group it

    Similar ideas combine into bigger projects, and each finds a home in something that already exists.

  3. 03

    Plan it

    What it should show and teach, and which official source each number comes from.

  4. 04

    Draw it first

    The design is drawn and judged before any of it is built.

  5. 05

    Build, check, ship

    Sources checked against their documentation, pages tested at every size, then published. Four tools went live on November 17.
